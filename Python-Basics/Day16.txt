match case:
-----------
	syntax
	------
	match expression:

		case value_1:
			stmt_1
			......
			stmt_n

		case value_2:
			stmt_1
			......
			stmt_n
		---------
		---------
		case _: #default case
		     stmt_1
		     ......
		     stmt_n

Note:
-----
this concept is introduced from python3.10 versions onwards.

ex1: elif ladder
----
	demo.py
	--------
pet=input("enter your pet: ")
if pet=='tiger':
    print(f"you pet is {pet}")
elif pet=='lion':
    print(f"you pet is {pet}")
elif pet=='cat':
    print(f"you pet is {pet}")
elif pet=='dog':
    print(f"you pet is {pet}")
else:
    print(f"you pet is {pet}")
    
	(or)

ex2: match case
----

	demo.py
	-------
pet=input("enter your pet: ")
match pet:
    case 'tiger':
        print(f"you pet is {pet}")
    case 'lion':
        print(f"you pet is {pet}")
    case 'cat':
        print(f"you pet is {pet}")
    case 'dog':
        print(f"you pet is {pet}")
    case _:
        print(f"you pet is {pet}")

	output1
	-------
enter your pet: cat
you pet is cat

	output2
	-------
enter your pet: dog
you pet is dog

	output3
	-------
enter your pet: lion
you pet is lion

	output4
	-------
enter your pet: tiger
you pet is tiger

	output5
	-------
enter your pet: parrot
you pet is parrot

Looping/Iterative Statements:
-------------------------------
the Looping/Iterative Statements are used to execute the block of statements repeatedly N no.of times.

the python supported Looping/Iterative Statements are,

	while loop
	while else
	for loop
	for else

while loop:
------------
the while loop is used to execute the block of statements repeatedly or repeated no.of times until while condition become Fail/False.

the while loop can be categorized into 2 type's, they are

	1).finite while loop

	2).infinite while loop

Finite while loop:
-------------------
Finite while loop means at any one point of time our while condition become Fail/False.

	syntax
	-------
	while condition:
		stmt_1
		stmt_2
		......
		stmt_n
