ex1:
----
write a python program to print '*'s in square pattern?

sample input:
	num=5

expected output:

* * * * *
* * * * *
* * * * *
* * * * *
* * * * *

	demo.py
	-------
num=int(input("enter your number: "))
r=1
while r<=num:
    c=1
    while c<=num:
        print("*",end=" ")
        c+=1
    r+=1
    print()

	(or)

num=int(input("enter your number: "))
for r in range(1,num+1):
    for c in range(1,num+1):
        print("*",end=" ")
    print()


ex2:
---
write a python program to print '*'s in hollow square pattern?

sample input:
	num=5

expected output:

* * * * *
*       *
*       *
*       *
* * * * *

	demo.py
	-------
num=int(input("enter your number: "))
r=1
while r<=num:
    c=1
    while c<=num:
        if r==1 or r==num or c==1 or c==num:
            print("*",end=" ")
        else:
            print(" ",end=" ")
        c+=1
    r+=1
    print()

	(or)

    for c in range(1,num+1):
        if r==1 or r==num or c==1 or c==num:
            print("*",end=" ")
        else:
            print(" ",end=" ")
    print()

    *

	demo.py
	-------
num=int(input("enter your number: "))

        print("*",end=" ")
    print()

	num=5

expected output:

    *
   * *
  * * *

* * * * * 
 * * * *
  * * *
   * *
    *

	demo.py
	-------
num=int(input("enter your number: "))
for r in range(1,num+1):
    for c in range(1,num-r+1):
        print(" ",end="")
    for c in range(1,r+1):
        print("*",end=" ")
    print()
for r in range(1,num):
    for c in range(1,r+1):
        print(" ",end="")
    for c in range(1,num-r+1):
        print("*",end=" ")
    print()
    
ex8:
----
write a python program to print '*'s in pyramid pattern?

sample input:

	num=5

expected output:

        *
      * * *
    * * * * *
  * * * * * * *
* * * * * * * * *

	demo.py
	-------
num=int(input("enter your number: "))
for r in range(1,num+1):
    
    for c in range(1,num-r+1):
        print(" ",end=" ")
    
    for c in range(1,r+1):
        print("*",end=" ")

    for c in range(1,r):
        print("*",end=" ")
    
    print()
